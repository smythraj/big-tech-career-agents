# Six-Month Google SWE Execution Roadmap

**Plan window:** 16 October 2026 – 16 April 2027  
**Primary outcome:** become application-ready and interview-ready for suitable Google Software Engineer roles, apply consistently, and improve from measured practice. An offer within six months is the target, not a guarantee; hiring depends on role fit, interview performance, headcount, and process timing.

## Starting strategy

Prioritize Google India roles aligned with experienced software engineering: Software Engineer II/III, Senior Software Engineer where the qualifications fit, Backend, Full Stack, Core, Infrastructure, and Google Cloud. A currently indexed Google Careers listing for Senior Software Engineer, Core in Bengaluru asks for 5 years of software development and 3 years testing/maintaining/launching products; verify every requirement on the live listing before applying. Avoid roles restricted to PhD candidates unless eligible.

Use Java as the primary interview language. The preparation loop is solve → explain → test edge cases → analyze complexity → re-solve after 3–7 days. Track actual work in the dashboard; agents must not infer completed tasks.

## Six-month milestones

| Month | Dates | Focus | Measurable exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 16 Oct–15 Nov 2026 | Baseline, arrays/strings, hash maps, two pointers, sliding window, binary search; Java collections and complexity; resume baseline | 25–35 quality problems, 2 timed mocks, resume v1, target-role matrix |
| 2 | 16 Nov–15 Dec 2026 | Linked lists, stacks/queues, trees, heaps, recursion/backtracking; Java concurrency and API design | 30–40 additional problems, 2 mocks, 5 STAR stories, first tailored applications |
| 3 | 16 Dec 2026–15 Jan 2027 | Graph BFS/DFS, topological sort, intervals, greedy, introductory DP; backend and database fundamentals | 30–40 additional problems, 2 coding mocks, 2 system-design walkthroughs, weekly applications/referral outreach |
| 4 | 16 Jan–15 Feb 2027 | DP patterns, advanced trees/graphs, system design fundamentals, distributed systems, caching, queues, consistency, reliability | 25–35 focused problems, 4 timed mocks, 4 system-design sessions, applications reviewed weekly |
| 5 | 16 Feb–15 Mar 2027 | Interview simulation, weak-area repair, Googleyness/behavioral practice, role-specific revision | 4 full mock loops, 8 polished STAR stories, revisit error log, maintain targeted application cadence |
| 6 | 16 Mar–16 Apr 2027 | Final readiness, recruiter follow-ups, interview loops, targeted applications and rapid feedback | 2 mocks/week, all high-frequency weak patterns revised, application pipeline reviewed twice weekly |

Problem counts are targets, not quotas to rush. Accuracy, explanation quality, and re-solving matter more than raw volume.

## Daily operating rhythm

**Core 90 minutes**
1. 10 min: retrieve yesterday's notes and one old mistake.
2. 45 min: one Java DSA problem under a timer; explain the approach before coding.
3. 15 min: test edge cases and state time/space complexity.
4. 15 min: review one Java/backend or system-design concept.
5. 5 min: log minutes, outcome, blocker, and next action in Career HQ.

**Optional 30–45 minutes:** resume/referral/application work or a second problem. On busy days, use a 25-minute minimum session rather than breaking the streak.

## Weekly review scorecard

- Focused study minutes and sessions actually logged.
- New problems solved, re-solves completed, and independent success rate.
- Weak patterns and repeated bug types.
- Java/backend/system-design topics practiced.
- Mock interview score and communication feedback.
- Suitable roles verified, applications submitted, referrals requested, and next follow-up dates.
- One specific adjustment for the next week.

## Application and research rules

- Prefer official Google Careers listings. Open each role and verify current status, location, level, minimum qualifications, and date before applying.
- Search daily for Google SWE/backend/full-stack roles in India and relevant engineering/hiring news. The daily research agent reports its timestamp and sources; news results are leads, not proof of a vacancy.
- Apply only when minimum qualifications are reasonably met; tailor resume bullets to scope, ownership, reliability, scale, and measurable impact.
- Keep a role pipeline with next action and follow-up date. Do not fabricate referrals, progress, interview results, or vacancy status.
- Public GitHub Pages content is visible to everyone. Do not put personal data, tokens, or private application details in repository files.

## Readiness gates

**By 15 Nov:** know baseline strengths/weaknesses and have a consistent study habit.  
**By 15 Dec:** explain common data structures and solve most easy/medium patterns independently.  
**By 15 Jan:** handle trees/graphs and introductory DP; resume and applications active.  
**By 15 Feb:** communicate system-design trade-offs and complete timed coding mocks.  
**By 15 Mar:** demonstrate consistent mock performance and close repeat mistakes.  
**By 16 Apr:** be actively applying/interviewing with a documented feedback loop and a revised plan based on real results.
